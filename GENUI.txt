FEATURES
Katex
math-inline: Renders equations within the flow of a sentence (e.g., evaluating limits like limx→0​xsinx​=1).
math-display: Breaks the text and renders the equation centered on its own line for complex, multi-step derivations.
Rough notation (and modify colors)
rough-highlight: A thick, semi-transparent yellow marker line behind the text. Excellent for defining key terms.
rough-circle: An animated red or blue circle drawn around the text. Perfect for pinpointing a specific error in a formula or highlighting a specific coordinate on a graph.
rough-underline: A hand-drawn squiggly line beneath the text. Less aggressive than a highlight, good for drawing attention to secondary details.
rough-box: A hand-drawn rectangle enclosing the text. Great for framing final answers or isolating a specific term.
rough-strike-through: A single hand-drawn line through the text. Useful for crossing out common misconceptions or incorrect multiple-choice options.
rough-crossed-off: A large "X" drawn over the text, indicating a completely invalid assumption.
rough-bracket: Hand-drawn brackets [ ] around a block of text, useful for grouping logical steps together.
Basic markdown
standard: The default, unformatted text.
bold: Used for core principles or final answers.
italic: Used for variables in a sentence or shifting tone.
code-inline: Monospaced font with a subtle background for code snippets or specific string references.

INTRICACIES
1. The "Legacy Shim"  What it is: Code to prevent old chat history from crashing when you change the architecture.  The Verdict: Trash it. If you don't care about preserving old database sessions, skip it entirely. You just saved yourself a massive headache and kept your codebase way cleaner.  2. maxSteps: 1  What it is: A safety switch for the AI.  The Verdict: Keep it. When the AI drops a multiple-choice question on the screen, you want it to shut up and wait for the student to click an answer. If you don't set maxSteps: 1, the AI might hallucinate and try to "answer" the MCQ itself in the background. This setting just forces the AI to hand the UI over to the user and pause.  3. Parallel Tool Calling (Seamless & Segmented)  What it is: The AI doing two things at once.  The Verdict: This is exactly what you want. It is the core of the "seamless and segmented" vibe. It allows the AI to stream standard conversational text (like a normal chat bubble or a bulleted list) and immediately segment the screen with a rich UI block (like a RoughNotation highlight, a blurred spoiler, or an MCQ) in the exact same response.
